GenLayer Bradbury testnet

See the code a GenLayer contract really runs

Rust contracts are too large for one Bradbury transaction, so they go on chain in pieces through a small loader. Explorers then show the loader, not the contract. This page deploys those contracts from your wallet and lets anyone prove which code is running.

1. Connect a wallet

Every transaction is signed in your wallet. This page never sees your key.

2. Choose the contract

3. Upload and lock

You confirm one transaction per piece. The contract is locked only after the uploaded bytes are read back and match your file.

    Chain data comes from the public RPC at rpc-bradbury.genlayer.com, so these results are only as honest as that node. Testnet only.
